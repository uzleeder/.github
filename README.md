# UzLeeder

### Technology. Intelligence. Innovation.

**Building meaningful technology from Uzbekistan for the world.**

UzLeeder is an AI-first technology studio founded in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. We build software, digital platforms, and intelligent solutions that help businesses solve real problems and operate more effectively.

## What We Do

- **AI Engineering** — Intelligent assistants, AI agents, RAG systems, and AI-powered applications.
- **Software Development** — Modern websites, web applications, and custom digital platforms.
- **Business Automation** — Intelligent workflows and operational automation.
- **Digital Innovation** — Research, experimentation, and development of scalable technology products.

## Our Projects

- **Space Zero Workspace** — A digital workspace initiative focused on collaboration, training, and organizational workflows.
- **T-Port** — A transport search platform concept connecting travelers with transportation options and official providers.
- **UzLeeder Academy** — A digital learning platform and educational initiative.
